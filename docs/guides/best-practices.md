# Best Practices for Project Management

## Task Management

### Creating Issues

**DO:**
- ✅ Use descriptive, action-oriented titles
- ✅ Provide clear acceptance criteria
- ✅ Add relevant labels and priority
- ✅ Estimate effort when possible
- ✅ Break large tasks into smaller ones

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Create vague or ambiguous issues
- ❌ Skip important details
- ❌ Forget to assign priority
- ❌ Create duplicate issues
- ❌ Make tasks too large (>1 week)

### Updating Issues

**DO:**
- ✅ Update status regularly
- ✅ Comment on progress
- ✅ Report blockers immediately
- ✅ Link related issues
- ✅ Close when complete

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Let issues go stale
- ❌ Work silently without updates
- ❌ Hide problems
- ❌ Leave issues orphaned
- ❌ Close without verification

## Sprint Planning

### Planning Phase

**DO:**
- ✅ Set a clear sprint goal
- ✅ Consider team capacity
- ✅ Prioritize ruthlessly
- ✅ Define done criteria
- ✅ Identify dependencies

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Overcommit the team
- ❌ Include unclear tasks
- ❌ Skip risk assessment
- ❌ Ignore past velocity
- ❌ Forget about time off

### During Sprint

**DO:**
- ✅ Hold daily standups
- ✅ Update task status
- ✅ Address blockers quickly
- ✅ Focus on sprint goal
- ✅ Help teammates

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Add scope mid-sprint
- ❌ Work in isolation
- ❌ Skip ceremonies
- ❌ Ignore warnings
- ❌ Abandon sprint goal

### Sprint Review

**DO:**
- ✅ Demo completed work
- ✅ Gather feedback
- ✅ Celebrate achievements
- ✅ Update documentation
- ✅ Track metrics

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Show incomplete work
- ❌ Skip retrospective
- ❌ Ignore lessons learned
- ❌ Blame team members
- ❌ Forget to improve

## Documentation

### Writing Documentation

**DO:**
- ✅ Keep it simple and clear
- ✅ Use consistent formatting
- ✅ Include examples
- ✅ Update regularly
- ✅ Make it searchable

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Write walls of text
- ❌ Use jargon excessively
- ❌ Forget to update
- ❌ Hide important info
- ❌ Duplicate content

### Meeting Notes

**DO:**
- ✅ Capture decisions
- ✅ List action items
- ✅ Note attendees
- ✅ Share promptly
- ✅ Follow up on actions

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Write verbatim transcripts
- ❌ Miss key decisions
- ❌ Forget action items
- ❌ Delay sharing notes
- ❌ Skip follow-up

## Communication

### Team Communication

**DO:**
- ✅ Be clear and concise
- ✅ Use appropriate channels
- ✅ Respond in timely manner
- ✅ Tag relevant people
- ✅ Be respectful

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Use unclear language
- ❌ Spam everyone
- ❌ Ignore messages
- ❌ Be passive-aggressive
- ❌ Make assumptions

### Reporting Status

**DO:**
- ✅ Report regularly
- ✅ Be honest about progress
- ✅ Highlight blockers
- ✅ Share wins
- ✅ Ask for help

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Hide problems
- ❌ Exaggerate progress
- ❌ Wait until too late
- ❌ Take all the credit
- ❌ Struggle alone

## Planning

### Short-term Planning

**DO:**
- ✅ Plan 1-2 sprints ahead
- ✅ Keep backlog groomed
- ✅ Refine estimates
- ✅ Adjust priorities
- ✅ Stay flexible

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Over-plan too far ahead
- ❌ Let backlog grow unchecked
- ❌ Stick to bad plans
- ❌ Ignore changing priorities
- ❌ Be too rigid

### Long-term Planning

**DO:**
- ✅ Define clear goals
- ✅ Create roadmap
- ✅ Identify milestones
- ✅ Review quarterly
- ✅ Communicate vision

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Set unrealistic goals
- ❌ Plan every detail
- ❌ Never revise plans
- ❌ Ignore market changes
- ❌ Keep plans secret

## Quality

### Code Quality

**DO:**
- ✅ Follow coding standards
- ✅ Write tests
- ✅ Review code
- ✅ Document code
- ✅ Refactor regularly

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Skip code review
- ❌ Ignore tests
- ❌ Write unclear code
- ❌ Leave tech debt
- ❌ Rush quality

### Process Quality

**DO:**
- ✅ Follow defined process
- ✅ Measure effectiveness
- ✅ Improve continuously
- ✅ Gather feedback
- ✅ Adapt as needed

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Blindly follow process
- ❌ Never measure
- ❌ Resist change
- ❌ Ignore feedback
- ❌ Stick to broken process

## Time Management

### Personal Time

**DO:**
- ✅ Set realistic deadlines
- ✅ Take breaks
- ✅ Focus on one task
- ✅ Minimize distractions
- ✅ Track time spent

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Overcommit
- ❌ Work unsustainably
- ❌ Multitask excessively
- ❌ Let interruptions rule
- ❌ Ignore burnout signs

### Team Time

**DO:**
- ✅ Run efficient meetings
- ✅ Respect time zones
- ✅ Have clear agendas
- ✅ Start and end on time
- ✅ Send pre-reads

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Hold unnecessary meetings
- ❌ Schedule back-to-back meetings
- ❌ Go off-topic
- ❌ Run over time
- ❌ Invite everyone

## Risk Management

### Identifying Risks

**DO:**
- ✅ List potential risks early
- ✅ Assess impact and probability
- ✅ Involve the team
- ✅ Update regularly
- ✅ Be realistic

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Ignore obvious risks
- ❌ Downplay severity
- ❌ Keep risks to yourself
- ❌ Forget to reassess
- ❌ Be overly pessimistic

### Mitigating Risks

**DO:**
- ✅ Create mitigation plans
- ✅ Assign risk owners
- ✅ Monitor indicators
- ✅ Act early
- ✅ Learn from issues

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Hope risks go away
- ❌ Make no one responsible
- ❌ Ignore warning signs
- ❌ React too late
- ❌ Repeat mistakes

---

Remember: These are guidelines, not rigid rules. Adapt them to your team's needs and context.
