# Project Manager Blindspots - Everint Project

**Last Updated:** January 30, 2026  
**Purpose:** Identify gaps, risks, and missing elements from a Project Manager perspective that may not be immediately visible in task lists or daily operations.

---

## 🎯 Strategic & Planning Blindspots

### Project Acceptance & Handover
- **Acceptance criteria clarity:** Are acceptance criteria clearly defined and measurable? What exactly constitutes "done" for each system?
- **Handover timeline:** Is there a clear handover date? What are the dependencies for handover completion?
- **Client expectations:** Are client expectations documented and aligned with project deliverables?
- **Success metrics:** Are KPIs clearly defined? How will success be measured post-handover?

### Resource Planning
- **Team capacity:** Are team members overloaded? Is there a clear view of who is working on what?
- **Skill gaps:** Are there critical skills missing? Do we have backup for key personnel?
- **Cross-training:** If a key person is unavailable, can others step in?
- **Resource allocation:** Are resources optimally allocated between PCB, Screw, and Rubber Foot robots?

---

## 📊 Data & Metrics Blindspots

### Performance Tracking
- **Cycle time tracking:** Are cycle times being systematically tracked and reported?
- **Success/failure rates:** Is there real-time visibility into success/failure rates?
- **Production throughput:** How many products are being processed per day/hour?
- **Downtime tracking:** How much downtime is occurring? What are the main causes?

### Quality Metrics
- **Vision model accuracy:** Are vision model accuracy metrics being tracked over time?
- **Error categorization:** Are errors being categorized and prioritized systematically?
- **Trend analysis:** Are we tracking trends (improving/worsening) for critical metrics?

---

## 🔗 Integration & Dependencies Blindspots

### System Integration
- **End-to-end testing:** Has the full production line been tested end-to-end?
- **Integration points:** Are all integration points between systems clearly defined and tested?
- **Data flow:** Is data flowing correctly between GUI, backend, vision, and robot systems?
- **MQTT communication:** Are all MQTT topics and messages documented and validated?

### External Dependencies
- **Hardware delivery:** Are all hardware items delivered? What's pending?
- **Supplier dependencies:** Are there dependencies on suppliers (e.g., rubber pad manufacturer, 3D printing)?
- **Client dependencies:** Are there any blockers waiting on client decisions or approvals?
- **Government requirements:** Are all government report requirements understood and on track?

## ⚠️ Risk Management Blindspots

### Technical Risks
- **Single points of failure:** Are there critical components with no backup or redundancy?
- **Technical debt:** Is technical debt being tracked and managed?
- **Scalability concerns:** Will the system scale if production volume increases?
- **Maintenance complexity:** How complex is the system to maintain post-handover?

### Operational Risks
- **Knowledge silos:** Is critical knowledge concentrated in one person?
- **Process gaps:** Are there gaps in processes (e.g., product registration, error handling)?
- **Communication gaps:** Are there communication gaps between teams or shifts?
- **Escalation paths:** Are escalation paths clear for critical issues?
